Receh kecil, tabungan besar. Small change, big savings.

PocketPay adalah dompet digital Android yang membulatkan setiap pembayaran QRIS dan menabung selisihnya untukmu. Ada kantong berbunga, refund 24 jam, statistik, dan database terenkripsi. PocketPay is an Android wallet that rounds up every QRIS payment and saves the difference for you. It adds interest-bearing pockets, 24-hour refunds, statistics and an encrypted database.

Cerita aplikasiThe app story

Satu pembayaran, lima hal yang terjadi di baliknyaOne payment, five things happening behind it

Gulir untuk mengikuti perjalanan satu transaksi. Layar di kanan berubah mengikuti setiap bab.Scroll to follow a single transaction. The screen on the right changes with every chapter.

Catatan: uangnya simulasi, dan QRIS-nya hanya valid secara format karena PocketPay bukan penerbit QRIS terdaftar. Jangan dipakai untuk dana sungguhan.Heads-up: the money is simulated and the QRIS codes are only structurally valid because PocketPay is not a registered QRIS issuer. Do not use it for real funds.
01

Bayar tanpa mikirPay without thinking

Pindai QRIS lewat kamera atau galeri. Hanya QRIS valid (CRC-16, mata uang IDR) yang diterima; kode lain langsung ditolak.Scan QRIS with the camera or the gallery. Only valid QRIS (CRC-16, IDR currency) is accepted; anything else is rejected immediately.

  • Pilih sumber dana: Rekening Utama atau kantong.Choose the source: the Main Account or a pocket.
  • Kantong kurang? Selisihnya otomatis diambil dari Rekening Utama dalam satu transaksi atomik.Pocket short? The difference is taken from the Main Account inside one atomic transaction.
02

Receh jadi tabunganSpare change becomes savings

Nominal dibulatkan ke atas ke kelipatan Rp 5.000 dan selisihnya masuk ke Celengan Receh. Kantong lain menumbuhkan uangmu dengan bunga.The amount is rounded up to the next Rp 5,000 and the difference goes to the Spare Change Jar. Other pockets grow your money with interest.

  • Kantong belanja 3,5% p.a. dengan target dan progres.Spending pockets at 3.5% p.a. with goals and progress.
  • Deposito 6% p.a. terkunci sampai jatuh tempo.Deposits at 6% p.a. locked until maturity.
  • Bunga harian masuk lewat WorkManager, walau aplikasi tertutup.Daily interest arrives through WorkManager even when the app is closed.
03

Lihat ke mana uangmu pergiSee where your money goes

Grafik mingguan dan bulanan dibangun dari transaksi nyata. Ketuk satu batang untuk melihat pemasukan dan pengeluaran hari itu.Weekly and monthly charts are built from real transactions. Tap a bar to see that day's income and spending.

  • Refund, pembayaran yang dibatalkan, dan perpindahan antar-kantong tidak dihitung ganda.Refunds, reversed payments and internal moves are never double-counted.
  • Riwayat bisa dicari dan difilter menurut tipe dan periode.History can be searched and filtered by type and period.
04

Aman sejak dibukaSafe from the first tap

Aplikasi selalu mulai terkunci. PIN 6 digit atau biometrik diminta lagi setelah 60 detik di latar dan sebelum setiap pembayaran.The app always starts locked. A 6-digit PIN or biometrics is asked again after 60 s in the background and before every payment.

  • Lima kali salah mengunci kolom PIN selama 30 detik.Five wrong tries lock PIN entry for 30 seconds.
  • Database SQLCipher; kuncinya dibungkus Android Keystore.SQLCipher database; its key is wrapped by the Android Keystore.
05

Ada bukti, bisa dibatalkanReceipts you can reverse

Setiap transaksi punya struk digital dengan nomor referensi dan kunci idempotensi, sehingga tidak pernah tercatat dua kali.Every transaction has a digital receipt with a reference number and an idempotency key, so it can never be recorded twice.

  • Refund dalam 24 jam: dana kembali, transaksi asli ditandai dibatalkan.Refund within 24 hours: money returns and the original is marked reversed.
  • Rekening koran PDF multi-halaman untuk dibuka atau dibagikan.A multi-page PDF statement to open or share.

KeamananSecurity

Lima lapis, dan jujur soal batasnyaFive layers, honest about the limits

1

LayarScreen

FLAG_SECURE menutup screenshot dan Recents. Aplikasi mulai terkunci dan terkunci lagi setelah 60 detik di latar.FLAG_SECURE blocks screenshots and Recents. The app starts locked and relocks after 60 s in the background.

2

AksesAccess

PIN 6 digit (PBKDF2, 120 rb iterasi; PIN lemah ditolak), kuncian 30 detik setelah 5 kali salah, dan biometrik terikat CryptoObject.A 6-digit PIN (PBKDF2, 120k iterations; weak PINs rejected), a 30 s lockout after 5 misses, and CryptoObject-bound biometrics.

3

Data diamData at rest

SQLCipher AES-256 dengan kata sandi acak yang dibungkus kunci Android Keystore. Database lama yang belum terenkripsi dikonversi otomatis.SQLCipher AES-256 with a random passphrase wrapped by an Android Keystore key. Older unencrypted databases are converted automatically.

4

TransaksiTransactions

Atomik, saldo dibaca ulang, kunci idempotensi, dan error bertipe yang tidak membocorkan detail internal.Atomic, balances re-read, idempotency keys, and typed errors that never leak internals.

5

PerangkatDevice

Tanpa backup, FileProvider hanya untuk folder rekening koran, R8 aktif, dan notifikasi privat di layar kunci.No backups, a FileProvider limited to the statements folder, R8 enabled, and private lock-screen notifications.

Yang belum adaWhat is not there

  • Tidak ada backend atau akun; perangkat adalah satu-satunya sumber data.No backend or accounts; the device is the only source of truth.
  • Tidak ada deteksi root/emulator (Play Integrity).No root/emulator detection (Play Integrity).
  • Penghitung salah-PIN ada di preferensi biasa, jadi bisa direset di perangkat root.PIN-attempt counters live in regular preferences, so a rooted device could reset them.
  • Jalur biometrik belum diuji di HP dengan sidik jari terdaftar.The biometric path has not been tested on a phone with an enrolled fingerprint.
  • QRIS valid secara format, tetapi PocketPay bukan penerbit terdaftar.QRIS is structurally valid, but PocketPay is not a registered issuer.

Mulai cepatQuick start

Jalankan dalam beberapa menitRun it in minutes

$ git clone https://github.com/dzakiabrar21/PocketPay.git $ cd PocketPay $ ./gradlew installDebug
  • Butuh Android Studio (JDK 17+) dan perangkat atau emulator Android 8.0+ (API 26).Needs Android Studio (JDK 17+) and an Android 8.0+ (API 26) device or emulator.
  • Saat pertama dibuka, buat PIN 6 digit (tidak boleh berulang atau berurutan).On first launch create a 6-digit PIN (no repeats or sequences).
  • Di emulator, pakai Input manual di pemindai karena tidak ada kamera.On an emulator use Enter manually in the scanner since there is no camera.
  • Data awal: Rekening Utama Rp 5.000.000 plus kantong Jajan Harian dan Deposito Darurat.Seed data: a Rp 5,000,000 Main Account plus Jajan Harian and Deposito Darurat pockets.
