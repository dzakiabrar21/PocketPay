Tentang proyekAbout

Prototipe yang jujur, dengan fitur yang benar-benar jalanAn honest prototype whose features really work

PocketPay dibuat untuk menunjukkan rekayasa aplikasi keuangan di Android: aturan uang yang atomik, penyimpanan terenkripsi, alur pembayaran lengkap, dan desain modern. Tidak ada server; semuanya tersimpan di perangkat. PocketPay showcases financial-app engineering on Android: atomic money rules, encrypted storage, a complete payment flow and a modern design. There is no server; everything lives on the device.

Penting: uang di aplikasi ini simulasi dan QRIS-nya hanya valid secara format (PocketPay bukan penerbit QRIS terdaftar). Jangan dipakai untuk dana sungguhan. Please note: the money in this app is simulated and its QRIS codes are only structurally valid (PocketPay is not a registered QRIS issuer). Do not use it for real funds.

Bayar QRISPay with QRIS

Pindai lewat kamera atau galeri. Hanya QRIS valid (CRC-16, IDR) yang diterima. Sumber dana berlapis: kekurangan kantong otomatis diambil dari Rekening Utama dalam satu transaksi atomik.Scan with the camera or the gallery. Only valid QRIS (CRC-16, IDR) is accepted. Cascading funding: a short pocket is topped up from the Main Account inside one atomic transaction.

Celengan recehRound-up savings

Pembayaran dibulatkan ke atas ke kelipatan Rp 5.000 dan selisihnya masuk ke Celengan Receh. Bisa dimatikan per pembayaran.Payments are rounded up to the next Rp 5,000 and the change goes into the Spare Change Jar. It can be switched off per payment.

Kantong tabunganSavings pockets

Kantong belanja (3,5% p.a.) dengan target opsional dan progres, serta deposito terkunci (6% p.a., 1/3/6 bulan) yang tidak bisa disentuh sebelum jatuh tempo.Spending pockets (3.5% p.a.) with optional goals and progress, and locked deposits (6% p.a., 1/3/6 months) that stay untouchable until maturity.

Bunga harian otomatisAutomatic daily interest

Bunga sederhana per hari dikreditkan lewat WorkManager walau aplikasi tidak dibuka. Dihitung dengan BigDecimal, tidak pernah dibayar dua kali.Simple daily interest is credited by WorkManager even when the app is closed. Computed with BigDecimal and never paid twice.

Refund & status transaksiRefunds & status

Batalkan pembayaran dalam 24 jam: dana kembali ke Rekening Utama, transaksi asli ditandai dibatalkan, dan refund kedua ditolak.Reverse a payment within 24 hours: money returns to the Main Account, the original is marked reversed and a second refund is rejected.

Statistik nyataReal statistics

Grafik mingguan dan bulanan dari transaksi sungguhan. Refund, pembayaran yang dibatalkan, dan perpindahan antar-kantong tidak dihitung ganda.Weekly and monthly charts built from real transactions. Refunds, reversed payments and internal moves are never double-counted.

Scan me & e-statementScan me & e-statement

Kode QR terima yang bisa dipindai dengan nominal opsional, dan rekening koran PDF multi-halaman yang bisa dibuka atau dibagikan.A scannable receive QR with an optional amount, and a multi-page PDF statement you can open or share.

Notifikasi pribadiPrivate notifications

Pemberitahuan deposito jatuh tempo, target tercapai, dan bunga masuk. Di layar kunci hanya tampil kalimat umum.Alerts for matured deposits, reached goals and credited interest. The lock screen only shows a generic line.

AturanRuleCara kerjaHow it works
Round-upNominal dibulatkan ke atas ke kelipatan Rp 5.000; selisih dipindahkan dari Rekening Utama ke Celengan Receh.The amount is rounded up to a multiple of Rp 5,000; the difference moves from the Main Account to the Spare Change Jar.
CascadingJika kantong kurang, selisihnya diambil dari Rekening Utama. Ditolak bila gabungan saldo tak cukup untuk pembayaran + round-up.If the pocket is short, the deficit is taken from the Main Account. Rejected if the combined balance cannot cover payment + round-up.
DepositoDepositTerkunci sampai jatuh tempo: tidak bisa membayar, ditransfer, ditarik, atau ditutup.Locked until maturity: cannot pay, be transferred, withdrawn or closed.
BungaInterestsaldo × rate ÷ 100 ÷ 365 × hari penuhwhole days — dibulatkan ke bawah, maks. 365 hari per proses, sisa hari terbawa.rounded down, at most 365 days per run, the partial day carries over.
Refund24 jam. Dana kembali ke Rekening Utama; asli → REVERSED; entri kredit "Refund"; round-up tetap tersimpan.24 hours. Funds return to the Main Account; original → REVERSED; a "Refund" credit entry; the round-up stays saved.
IdempotensiIdempotencyUUID per pembayaran sebagai kunci transaksi (ABORT bila ganda); pembayaran kedua diabaikan saat satu sedang berjalan.A UUID per payment is the transaction key (ABORT on duplicates); a second payment is ignored while one is in flight.
IntegritasIntegritySaldo Long; nominal harus > 0; saldo dibaca ulang di dalam transaksi sehingga layar basi tidak bisa boros.Long balances; amounts must be > 0; balances are re-read inside the transaction so a stale screen cannot overspend.
KeamananSecurity

Berlapis, dan jujur soal batasannyaLayered, and honest about its limits

Perlindungan di perangkat yang sudah diterapkan, beserta yang belum.The on-device protections that are in place, and the ones that are not.

Sudah diterapkanImplemented

  • Database terenkripsi SQLCipher AES-256; kata sandi acak dibungkus kunci Android Keystore.Encrypted database: SQLCipher AES-256 with a random passphrase wrapped by an Android Keystore key.
  • PIN 6 digit, hash PBKDF2 (120 rb iterasi) yang dienkripsi lagi dengan Keystore; PIN lemah ditolak.PIN: 6 digits, PBKDF2 hash (120k iterations) further encrypted with the Keystore; weak PINs rejected.
  • Biometrik terikat kripto (CryptoObject); kunci tidak berlaku bila ada biometrik baru.Crypto-bound biometrics (CryptoObject); the key is invalidated when new biometrics are enrolled.
  • Kunci otomatis saat dibuka dingin dan 60 detik di latar; FLAG_SECURE menutup screenshot.Auto-lock on cold start and after 60 s in the background; FLAG_SECURE blocks screenshots.
  • Tanpa backup, FileProvider sempit, R8 aktif, notifikasi privat.No backups, a narrow FileProvider, R8 enabled, private notifications.

Belum / keterbatasanNot yet / limitations

  • Tidak ada backend atau akun: perangkat adalah satu-satunya sumber data.No backend or accounts: the device is the only source of truth.
  • Tidak ada deteksi root/emulator (Play Integrity).No root/emulator detection (Play Integrity).
  • Penghitung salah-PIN tersimpan di preferensi biasa.PIN-attempt counters live in regular preferences.
  • Jalur biometrik belum diuji di HP dengan sidik jari terdaftar.The biometric path has not been tested on a phone with an enrolled fingerprint.
  • QRIS valid secara format, tetapi PocketPay bukan penerbit terdaftar.QRIS is structurally valid, but PocketPay is not a registered issuer.
ArsitekturArchitecture

Clean Architecture + MVI, satu arahClean Architecture + unidirectional MVI

UI mengirim intent, ViewModel mengelola StateFlow, repository memanggil DAO yang menjalankan aturan uang secara atomik.The UI sends intents, the ViewModel owns a StateFlow, and the repository calls a DAO that runs the money rules atomically.

Keputusan desainDesign decisions

  • Semua operasi uang adalah satu transaksi Room; saldo dibaca ulang di dalamnya.Every money operation is one Room transaction, re-reading balances inside it.
  • Error bertipe (BankingError) dipetakan ke pesan dwibahasa; galat tak dikenal tidak membocorkan detail.Typed errors (BankingError) map to bilingual messages; unknown errors never leak details.
  • Layar tanpa state; overlay menelan sentuhan dan menyembunyikan layar di bawahnya dari TalkBack.Stateless screens; overlays swallow touches and hide what is beneath from TalkBack.
  • Warna sebagai peran semantik sehingga mode terang/gelap tak butuh hex di layar.Colours are semantic roles, so light/dark needs no hex values in screens.
